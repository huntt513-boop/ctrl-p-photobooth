HOW CTRL+P WORKS

1. Three-Shot Sequence: You will take 3 continuous poses with a countdown timer. You can review and retake any shot individually.

2. Pure B&W Dithering: All photographs are processed with a thermal dither algorithm optimized for black-and-white print quality on 57mm mini thermal printers like the LIOTOG C19.

3. Monochrome Stickers: Decorate your photo strip layout using touch-and-drag monochrome stickers.

4. Dual Output: Print directly to Bluetooth thermal printers, save high-res PNG strips, or scan the generated QR code to download digital gallery copies on your phone.

C19 PRINTING GUIDE
Method A: Companion App (Fun Print / WalkPrint)
  1. Tap "STRIP PNG" below to save the strip image to your iPad/device gallery.
  2. Open the Fun Print or WalkPrint app connected to your C19 printer.
  3. Select "Picture Print" -> choose saved CTRL+P strip PNG -> Print!
Method B: Web Bluetooth Direct

Click "BLUETOOTH PRINT" to pair with supported ESC/POS thermal printers directly from Chrome/iPad web browser.
